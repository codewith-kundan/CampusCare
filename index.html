About CampusCare

Building a better campus together.

CampusCare gives students a transparent, convenient way to report campus issues — and follow them through to resolution.

From hostel and mess facilities to electricity, connectivity, cleanliness and infrastructure, every complaint is registered, routed and tracked from one place.
