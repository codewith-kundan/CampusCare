SERVICES & DEPARTMENTS

Solve campus problems swiftly.

CampusCare directly connects students with designated chief wardens, estate maintenance teams, network admins, and academic coordinators for rapid action.

01

Hostel Maintenance

Room fixtures, plumbing, water supply, washroom repair, carpentry, and hall facility upkeep.

02

03

Electricity & Power

RESOLUTION PROCESS

How CampusCare Works

Four transparent steps from registration to verified resolution.

01

Submit with Evidence

Describe the issue, set the priority level, tag your hall/location, and attach photo proof.

02

Review & Triage

The complaint is verified by the administration team and routed to the corresponding department.

03

Action & In Progress

Maintenance workers or faculty resolve the issue on the ground and record timestamped updates.

04

Verified Closure

You receive immediate status confirmation on your dashboard with complete audit history.
